# Lone Wolf Action Chart

A pocket companion for **Lone Wolf Books 1–20**. Autosave your current adventure, carry your character forward, and use simple arithmetic helpers while you read and play.

[Open the Action Chart](https://sigfriedjk.github.io/lonewolf-action-chart/index.html)

Six tabs cover Stats, Kai training, Backpack, Special Items, Combat, and Journal. The chart supports Kai, Magnakai, and Grand Master training; book-specific starting supplies; safekeeping; combat rounds; and player-selected mastery, psychic, missile, and recovery helpers.

Changes to the current book save automatically in your browser. **Copy save link** creates a portable snapshot of the entire current chart, including its Journal and combat record. Copy it to another device, a bookmark, or your notes. Links do not update after you copy them; very large charts use a save file instead. Anyone with a link can read its contents.

Campaign history is optional. Keep named checkpoints from the adventure menu, or leave “Keep a checkpoint” checked when starting another book. Checkpoints stay unchanged as you play. Export the current book as a file, or export the current book and checkpoints together as an archive. Existing campaigns migrate into checkpoints, and older save files remain importable. Restoring a link, file, or checkpoint previews the chart before replacing current progress and can keep a checkpoint of the outgoing chart.

Undo restores recent changes, including checkpoint changes, during the current session. There is no account or cloud sync.

## Direction

The goal is an electronic paper Action Chart: useful recordkeeping and arithmetic, with the player interpreting and enforcing the book's rules. Training improvements and special abilities stay under your control. Section effects, eligibility, elapsed time, and unusual encounters remain player decisions, supported by editable scores and Journal notes.

Keep the six-tab interface compact and approachable on phones and desktops. Grand Master charts show all ten backpack slots on one page; smaller screens may scroll. Books 21 onward, a book reader, accounts, cloud sync, and an offline/PWA system are outside the current scope.
