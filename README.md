# Lone Wolf Action Chart

A pocket companion for **Lone Wolf Books 1–20**. Keep an independent character chart for each adventure, carry your character forward, and use simple arithmetic helpers while you read and play.

[Open the Action Chart](https://sigfriedjk.github.io/lonewolf-action-chart/index.html)

Six tabs cover Stats, Kai training, Backpack, Special Items, Combat, and Journal. The chart supports Kai, Magnakai, and Grand Master training; book-specific starting supplies; safekeeping; combat rounds; and player-selected mastery, psychic, missile, and recovery helpers.

Changes save in your browser. Export/import moves the whole campaign between browsers or devices and provides a backup. Undo restores recent changes during the current session. There is no account or cloud sync.

## Direction

The goal is an electronic paper Action Chart: useful recordkeeping and arithmetic, with the player interpreting and enforcing the book's rules. Training improvements and special abilities stay under your control. Section effects, eligibility, elapsed time, and unusual encounters remain player decisions, supported by editable scores and Journal notes.

Keep the six-tab interface compact and approachable on phones and desktops. Grand Master charts show all ten backpack slots on one page; smaller screens may scroll. Books 21 onward, a book reader, accounts, cloud sync, and an offline/PWA system are outside the current scope.
