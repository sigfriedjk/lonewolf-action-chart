## Approved Sun Lord combat helpers — implemented

Jon approved both choices after the Book 16 proposal:

- `grandFlaming` is a saved player-controlled check in Mastery, defaulting to false when missing. A successful Fight round (positive normal enemy loss) adds up to 1 enemy EP loss, capped by remaining EP. It adds nothing on evasion or a zero-damage table result. The player decides whether the weapon is eligible. The bonus is enemy damage, never CS, and is not multiplied by the Sommerswerd undead helper.
- `psychicMode: 'kaiBlast'` replaces other psychic attacks, gives no psychic CS bonus, and costs up to 4 EP per used round. Two additional entered/random numbers supply 2–18 enemy EP loss, with zero counted as one. Damage accompanies the normal round, is capped by remaining enemy EP, and is not doubled as Sommerswerd damage. Immunity suppresses the selected attack's damage; the player follows section-specific exceptions. As with the existing selected surge, evasion still includes its EP cost and ignores enemy loss. No cooldown or automatic attack switching is introduced.
- Round history optionally records `flameLoss`, `blastRolls`, `blastLoss`, and `blastCost`, all included within the displayed total losses. Validation retains them; cost/loss caps and Undo/reload/export/import are covered by tests. The existing optional `surgeLoss` remains separate. Old non-Grand charts keep their existing values/behavior.

## Grand Crown and Sun Prince choices — approved and implemented

Mastery now offers inherited unarmed training, Grand Master zero penalty, or Grand Crown +3 CS. The `grandUnarmed` record retains its earlier false/true values and adds the explicit string `crown`. It owns the replacement unarmed value; mastery/equipment bonuses are not added to unarmed attacks. Existing zero-penalty selections stay selected. No count, rank, or book number changes the choice.

Kai-ray is an action for one Fight round in the Psychic dialog. It uses an entered/random normal combat number, suppresses the usual psychic CS bonus/cost for that round, adds up to 15 enemy EP loss, and costs up to 4 EP. The usual `psychicMode` is retained. The dialog shows the resulting CS/ratio and reminds the player of more than 10 EP and once-per-combat use. These remain player decisions; there is no use counter or gate. The encounter's immunity setting applies to the ray too. Ray damage is capped, is not multiplied by Sommerswerd, and appears as optional `rayLoss`/`rayCost` fields within round totals. Undo reverses the whole action, and history survives reload/export/import.
